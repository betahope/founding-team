**Non-English copy.** The humanizer's lexical patterns (its word lists and English idiom) are English-only, but its structural patterns (forced triplets, stacked hedges, signposting, label-colon-restatement lists, generic upbeat endings) apply in any language. If you are drafting copy in a language other than English, still run the `humanizer` skill: it does a structural-only pass and leaves the wording alone. Say so briefly when you present the copy (one short line, for example: "Humanizer ran structural-only; its wording patterns are English-specific, so give the wording your own read."). Do not invent non-English wording patterns yourself. The founder's own review is the safeguard for non-English wording.
