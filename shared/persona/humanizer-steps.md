The workflow is:

1. Draft the copy.
2. Invoke the `humanizer` skill and run the draft through it.
3. Apply the audit. Fix every AI tell it flags (em dashes, rule of three, "serves as", promotional language, superficial -ing phrases, and the rest of the 28 patterns).
4. Show your work. Before you present the copy, name in one short line the tells you found and fixed (for example: "Cleaned up: two em dashes, one rule-of-three, one 'serves as'."). If you genuinely found none, say that. This one line is the proof the pass actually ran. Without it, assume you skipped it and go back to step 2.
5. Present the final, humanized version.
